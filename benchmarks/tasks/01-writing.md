# Task 01: reading-focused writing assistant

Include light/dark themes and a working composer preview without external APIs.

Use no brand logos/fonts or borrowed screenshots. Do not assume a real LLM is connected. Avoid unrelated management dashboards.

Deliver runnable files and browser validation at desktop and 390px.
